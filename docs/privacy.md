# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 30, 2026_

TrackMate (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to providing a safe, strictly confidential space for your personal records. This Privacy Policy explains our on-device approach to data storage and how your information is safeguarded when you use the TrackMate app.

---

## 1. Information Storage & Syncing

- **Local-First Storage** By default, all of your logged interactions, selected emotion tags, notes, and red-flag data are stored strictly on your device using Apple's Core Data architecture. We do not collect or transmit your entries to any external TrackMate servers.
  
- **Optional iCloud Syncing** If you enable iCloud for TrackMate, Apple will securely sync your data across your devices using your Apple ID. All syncing behavior and cloud storage are managed entirely by Apple’s systems and are governed by Apple’s privacy and security policies. 

- **Device Permissions** If you choose to use features that rely on notifications (such as daily toxic behavior reminders), the app will request permission to deliver them. Notification permissions can be changed at any time in your device settings.

---

## 2. On-Device Analysis & Machine Learning

- **Local Pattern Detection** TrackMate features automated pattern detection to help identify frequent toxic behaviors and interactions. This analysis is performed entirely locally on your device. Your interaction notes are never sent to external AI servers, third-party APIs, or our developers for processing.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

- **Zero Third-Party Sharing** We do not transmit, share, rent, or sell your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.
- **No Ads or Analytics** We do not collect analytics about your specific entries, and the app contains no advertisements.
- **Law Enforcement & Legal Requests** Because TrackMate is a serverless application and we do not collect user account data or metadata, we possess no user data to disclose to law enforcement. Any data you choose to sync via iCloud is held by Apple and is subject to Apple's law enforcement guidelines. 

---

## 4. Your Choices & Control

- **Data Deletion** You retain total control over your data. You may permanently delete your data at any time by removing individual entries within the app or by completely uninstalling the application.
- **Data Exporting** You will soon be able to natively export your data at any time directly from the app settings.
- **Diagnostic Opt-In** We do not receive crash reports or usage telemetry unless you explicitly opt in via your iOS device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements) or through Apple's TestFlight program.

---

## 5. Security

Your data stays on-device by default and is protected by iOS's built-in, industry-standard encryption. We rely on Apple’s Secure Enclave for device authentication (such as Face ID or device passcode) to protect your local database at rest.
